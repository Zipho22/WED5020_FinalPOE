About Gauteng Higher Learning Institution

Gauteng Higher Learning Institution is committed to providing quality, career-focused education in a supportive learning environment. Our goal is to prepare students for academic success and meaningful careers.

  1. Our History

    Gauteng Higher Learning Institution is a private higher learning institution established in 2011. The institution was founded to provide affordable, career-focused education for students wishing to develop practical skills in Business, Information Technology and Education. Since its establishment, the institution has grown to approximately 1,800 students and continues to prepare graduates for successful careers.

  2. Our Mission

    Our mission is to provide accessible, affordable and career-focused tertiary education that equips students with the knowledge, practical skills and confidence required to succeed in today's workplace.

  3. Our Vision

    Our vision is to become one of Gauteng's leading higher learning institutions recognised for academic excellence, innovation and producing work-ready graduates.

  4. Our Target Audience

    We welcome school leavers, working professionals seeking further education and organisations looking to develop the skills of their employees through quality higher education programmes.
